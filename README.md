**Overview**

Offshore installations are remote workplaces. Shifts can be long, tasks can be physical and medical support is not as close as it would be onshore. For this reason, employers and operators expect personnel to meet recognised medical fitness standards before they travel. Understanding the process early prevents delays, rescheduling and last-minute stress. 🛢️
This guide covers the OEUK Offshore Medical, what it involves and how to prepare.

**Why Standards Exist**

If a health issue arises offshore, response options are limited. Medical fitness standards help confirm that the people heading to an installation are healthy enough for the conditions they will face. This protects the individual, colleagues and the wider operation.

**The Assessment**

The assessment reviews general health and fitness for offshore conditions. While every assessment follows the relevant standard, a visit commonly includes:

✅ Review of medical history
✅ Physical examination and vital signs check
✅ Vision and hearing assessment
✅ Additional tests as required by the examination standard

The doctor explains what is being checked and why, and you can ask questions at any point. 🩺
Note: Requirements can differ between employers and operators. Always confirm which standard your company expects before booking.

**Preparation**

A little preparation makes the appointment smoother:
Bring valid identification. 🪪
Bring medical records. Include previous reports, test results and a list of current medication.
Confirm the standard. Ask your employer which medical they require.
Write down questions. The visit is a good time to raise any health concerns.
Allow buffer time. If the doctor recommends follow-up tests, you will want time to complete them before mobilisation.

**For Crew Managers**

If you send crews offshore regularly, a missing certificate can disrupt rotations. A few habits help:
Keep a register of medical dates and expiry times
Schedule group appointments well ahead of rotations
Remind personnel to bring identification and records
Store documentation securely and make it easy to retrieve

A clinic that keeps appointments organised and documents clear helps your operations team plan with fewer surprises.
After the Assessment
Your doctor will explain the outcome and, where appropriate, recommend next steps. Keep your documents somewhere easy to find. Employers, agents and operators may ask to see them, and a digital backup is useful. 🌊
